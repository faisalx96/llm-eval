Variant notes

V0Original
The first pass — 5 tabs (Executive Brief, Pulse, Quality Radar, Cost, Team). Shows the full breadth of widgets before the critique. Baseline for comparison.
V1Polished
Same 5-tab structure, surgical polish only. Tab numbers removed, decorative card dots only when they signal state, sparklines inside the leaderboard, horizontal heatmap labels, a status banner up top, explicit period deltas, primary "New Run" CTA.
V2Consolidated
Dropped from 5 to 3 tabs: Overview / Models / Activity. Alerts, top movers, and regressions collapsed into one "Needs your attention" signals inbox. Efficiency Frontier promoted to hero on the Models tab. Cost folded into Models because it's the same decision.
V3Scroll
No tabs at all. Sticky anchor nav (Health / Attention / Models / Tasks / Cost / Team) with scroll-spy — reads top-to-bottom like a daily briefing. Efficiency Frontier is the centerpiece on the Models anchor. Best for executives who scroll, not click.
